# Shadow FX Learning Hub: Risk Management

## Protect your account
Risk management helps limit losses. It cannot remove trading risk.

- Choose the maximum amount you are willing to lose before a trade.
- Set a stop-loss based on your plan, not on hope.
- Understand that gaps, slippage, and execution can cause a different result from the planned loss.
- Avoid increasing trade size to recover a loss.
- Do not use borrowed money or money needed for essentials.

## Risk-to-reward
Risk-to-reward compares the possible loss with the planned possible gain. A 1:2 plan aims for a potential gain twice as large as the planned risk, but it does not mean the trade is likely to win.

## After every trade
Write down why you entered, where the plan was invalidated, whether you followed your rules, and what you learned.
