# Shadow FX Learning Hub: Chart Structure

## Read the chart
- **Swing high:** a local peak where price turned down.
- **Swing low:** a local low where price turned up.
- **Higher high (HH):** a swing high above a previous swing high.
- **Higher low (HL):** a swing low above a previous swing low.
- **Lower high (LH):** a swing high below a previous swing high.
- **Lower low (LL):** a swing low below a previous swing low.
- **Breakout:** price moves beyond a level traders are watching.

## Simple practice routine
1. Mark recent swing highs and lows.
2. Decide whether structure looks bullish, bearish, or unclear.
3. Wait for the candle to close before judging a break.
4. Plan where the idea would be wrong.
5. Review the chart afterwards. Do not treat one pattern as a guarantee.
