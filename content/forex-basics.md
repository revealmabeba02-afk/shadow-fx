# Shadow FX Learning Hub: Forex Basics

## What is forex?
Forex means foreign exchange. Traders buy one currency and sell another.

## Currency pairs
- EUR/USD: euro compared with the US dollar.
- GBP/USD: British pound compared with the US dollar.
- XAUUSD: gold priced in US dollars (often shown by trading platforms as a symbol).

## Key words
- **Pip:** a small unit used to measure price movement. The exact value depends on the pair and broker.
- **Spread:** the difference between the buy and sell price.
- **Lot:** the trade size.
- **Stop-loss:** an order intended to close a trade if price reaches a chosen loss level.
- **Take-profit:** an order intended to close a trade at a chosen profit level.

## Beginner rules
1. Learn the platform before risking money.
2. Practise on a demo account.
3. Decide your stop-loss before entering.
4. Never assume a setup must win.
5. Keep a journal of your decisions.
